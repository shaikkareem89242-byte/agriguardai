HOW TO TREAT IT?

Agronomic Action Plan & Dosage Safety

1. Immediate Action
  • Prune and safely burn or bury heavily infected lower leaves.
  • Isolate infected patch to stop spore dispersal.
2. Recommended Treatment

Copper-Based Fungicide or Organic Neem Oil

Apply broad-spectrum copper oxychloride or bio-fungicide solution thoroughly covering lower leaf surfaces.

3. Preventive Measures
  • Transition from overhead sprinkler to ground drip irrigation.
  • Maintain 60cm row spacing for canopy ventilation.
4. Things to Avoid
  • Do NOT apply nitrogenous fertilizer during active fungal outbreak.
  • Avoid working in wet fields to prevent spore mechanical transfer.
