Agronomic Treatment Plan

⚡

Immediate Action (Today)

Prune infected lower leaves that touch the soil. Safely burn or bury diseased leaves away from healthy crops. Avoid touching healthy plants after handling infected leaves.

🧪

Recommended Treatment Category

Apply copper-based organic fungicide or Mancozeb/Chlorothalonil broad-spectrum protective spray during the recommended weather window.

🛡️ Preventive & Irrigation

  • Switch from overhead sprinklers to drip irrigation to keep foliage dry.
  • Ensure adequate plant spacing to boost canopy air circulation.
  • Apply neem oil formulation as a bio-fungicide preventive barrier.

🚫 Things to Avoid

  • Do NOT spray during midday heat (>32°C) to prevent leaf scorching.
  • Do NOT spray when rain is expected within 4 hours.
  • Do NOT over-apply excess nitrogen fertilizers which soften leaf tissue.
